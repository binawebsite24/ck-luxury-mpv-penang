Good to know

Clear terms, no surprises

Minimum 8 hours

Shorter bookings are charged at the 8-hour rate — the car and driver are held for you regardless.

No split hours

A package runs continuously. Hours can't be used in the afternoon and again at night, and unused hours don't carry forward.

Package is fixed once confirmed

If the job finishes early the rate stays the same, because the car and driver were allocated for that duration.

Overtime

RM70–RM120 per hour depending on the vehicle. Airport transfers are RM75 per extra 30 minutes.

Peak season

A RM100 per day surcharge applies during super-peak periods.

Timing & payment

Hours run from hotel or airport pick-up to drop-off, rounded to the nearest 30 minutes. Payment is cash.

FAQ

Questions we get every week

Is the day package really all-in?+

Yes. Fuel, tolls and parking are included in the 8, 10 and 12-hour packages. Overtime and the peak-season surcharge are the only extras, and we tell you about them upfront.

Can I book for less than 8 hours?+

You can, but it is charged at the 8-hour rate. The vehicle and driver are reserved for you for the day either way.

Do you do self-drive?+

On selected vehicles. The Alphard AGH30 is RM650 a day and the Vellfire AGH40 is RM1,100 a day. Message us for what is free on your dates.

Can you drive us outside Penang?+

Yes — Ipoh, Taiping, Kulim, Alor Setar and Sungai Petani regularly, and Thailand or Singapore on request. A daily add-on applies, so send us the itinerary for a quote.

How do I pay?+

Cash. We will confirm the full amount on WhatsApp before your trip.

What if my flight is delayed?+

Tell us as soon as you know. Each airport package includes a waiting allowance, and beyond that overtime is RM75 per 30 minutes. We are contactable 24 hours.

Can you supply a car for a wedding?+

Yes — luxury MPVs, classic and vintage cars, and selected supercars including Porsche, Lamborghini, Ferrari, Bentley and McLaren. These are quoted per event.
